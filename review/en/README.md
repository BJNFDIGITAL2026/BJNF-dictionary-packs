# English review sheet

Rank, word, number of times seen in the corpus. A native speaker should mark wrong spellings, offensive words and personal names here before the pack is merged to `main`.

- `top_500.tsv` - most common words
- `random_300.tsv` - random sample across the whole list
- `tail_200.tsv` - the rarest words that still made the cut (most likely to contain errors)
